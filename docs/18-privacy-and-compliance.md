# 18 — Privacy and Compliance

**Not legal advice.** Verify current Bangladesh privacy/data, cyber-security, consumer, child-safety, payment, tax and electronic transaction requirements with qualified local counsel and relevant providers. Do not assert compliance based only on this document.

Inventory/classify public, internal, confidential and highly sensitive data. Minimize collection; explain purpose/access; record consent and policy version; define retention/deletion/export; protect tutor identity evidence and child records; restrict private files; redact logs/analytics; encrypt in transit/at rest; review subprocessors and data-transfer regions.

Legal review needed for age/guardian consent, identity/background checks, electronic transactions, consumer refunds, payment/fund handling, tax/invoices, messaging consent, record retention and incident notification. For every source record issuing body, title, effective/publication date, official URL, review date and counsel interpretation.
