# 02 — Business Model and Retention

## Models to test

1. Commission on completed bookings.
2. Optional tutor subscription for scheduling, reports, homework and receipts.
3. Coaching organization subscription if team workflows are validated.
4. Clearly labeled promotion that never masquerades as quality ranking.

All prices are hypotheses. Interview tutors and guardians, test transparent options, and measure conversion, churn, support cost, provider fees, refund rate, and acquisition cost. Never imply that paid verification means a tutor is safer.

## Ethical retention

Offer reliable booking records, service agreements, learning continuity, tutor tools, receipts, clear dispute/replacement processes, reminders, guardian access, and human support. Do not impose deceptive lock-in, abusive penalties, or withhold user-owned educational records.

## Local launch

Interview families/tutors; recruit a small quality cohort across subjects/classes; document proportionate verification; onboard guardians; manually monitor first bookings; review feedback weekly; expand only when response times, completed lessons, repeat usage and support capacity are healthy.
