# 27 — Business Policies

Policies require operations and qualified local legal review before publication.

**Verification:** accepted evidence, reviewer training, expiration/recheck, appeal, suspension and exact badge wording. Verification only means the checks actually performed.

**Cancellation/no-show:** time windows, fee logic, exceptions, evidence, dispute path and fee responsibility. Store policy version on booking.

**Replacement:** eligibility, timeframe, matching effort, alternatives, exclusions and escalation. Do not promise unlimited replacement without capacity.

**Reviews:** eligible completed service only, abuse detection, moderation/appeal and no paid review manipulation.

**Disputes:** intake, acknowledgement, evidence, response SLA, escalation, decision authority, appeal and permitted financial holds.

**Off-platform:** communicate benefits/fees clearly; use fair terms reviewed by counsel; never withhold user-owned learning records. Define support hours and urgent safety escalation.
