# Decisions to Resolve

1. Age/guardian consent and legal interpretation.
2. Coaching organizations in MVP or later.
3. Tutor verification evidence, review SLA, expiry and appeal.
4. Payment providers, merchant eligibility, fees, settlement, refunds and sandbox.
5. Whether platform holds funds or provider settles to tutors.
6. Commission and accounting treatment.
7. Cancellation/no-show/refund matrix.
8. Replacement eligibility and promise.
9. Recurring schedule and timezone edge cases.
10. Overlap behavior for pending/cancelled/reschedule sessions.
11. Retention/deletion and backup policy.
12. Storage region, malware scanning and signed URLs.
13. Messaging scope and minor safety.
14. Hosting region, budget, RTO/RPO and pilot volume.
15. Whether admin impersonation is needed (default: omit).
16. Analytics privacy boundaries.
17. Exact stable versions and current provider pricing.
