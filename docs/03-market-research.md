# 03 — Market Research Plan

This is a research plan, not a verified market report. Do not invent market size, pricing, legal facts or adoption rates. Review current tutor marketplaces, coaching-management tools, informal social groups and online education services. Potential Bangladesh references such as Caretutors and other tutoring services must be independently checked for current features, terms, fees, reviews and safety claims.

Interview guardians across class bands, independent admission candidates, tutors, coaching operators and local education stakeholders. Ask about current discovery, trust, fees, cancellations, payment habits, scheduling friction, records, and willingness to pay.

For each finding record date, source/URL or interview method, sample size, observation, limitations, confidence and affected decision. Distinguish stated interest from actual repeat use and payment behavior.
