# Architecture Decisions Needed & Resolutions

**Date:** 2026-10-10  
**Project:** OgroniEdu — Bangladesh Tutoring and Educational Management Platform  
**Status:** Approved & Baselined for Phase 0

---

## 1. Resolution of the 17 Core Implementation Decisions

In the original blueprint, `docs/32-implementation-decisions-to-resolve.md` left 17 fundamental business and technical decisions unresolved. The following definitive architectural decisions have been made and codified:

| #   | Question / Decision Area                      | Definitive Architectural Resolution                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Rationale & Trade-offs                                                                                                       |
| --- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Age & Guardian Consent Policy**             | Strict binary model: Learners under 18 years old must be registered and managed under a verified Guardian account. Learners 18+ (e.g. HSC 2nd year, University Admission, Job Seekers) can operate as Independent Students. Guardians have read/approval rights over bookings, payments, and progress for minor children.                              | Protects minors in accordance with Bangladesh ICT Act & global child safety standards without blocking adult exam examinees. |
| 2   | **Coaching Centers / Institutions**           | Deferred to Phase 2 (post-pilot). MVP focuses exclusively on 1-on-1 and small group private tutors (online and offline). Tutors can be independent practitioners or university students.                                                                                                                                                               | Reduces MVP complexity; focuses on core tutor-guardian-student matching and learning continuity.                             |
| 3   | **Tutor Verification Evidence, SLA & Appeal** | Multi-tier verification: (1) Identity: National ID (NID) or Birth Certificate / Student ID; (2) Academic: University certificate or enrollment ID card; (3) Review SLA: 48 hours by admin reviewer. Rejection includes an explicit reason code and an appeal window of 14 days with revised document upload.                                           | High trust and safety for guardians, transparent process for tutors.                                                         |
| 4   | **Payment Providers & Sandbox**               | Primary gateway: SSLCOMMERZ (supporting bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Visa, Mastercard, DBBL Nexus) with fallback to direct bKash Merchant API. Test sandbox credentials configured in `.env.example`. All webhook callbacks verified via server-to-server cryptographic hash & IPN IP validation.                                                             | Ubiquitous in Bangladesh; covers >98% of digital consumer payments.                                                          |
| 5   | **Fund Flow & Escrow / Settlement**           | Platform holds funds in escrow until the lesson/session milestone is completed. Upon session completion (or after a 24-hour dispute dispute-free window), funds are unlocked into the Tutor's ledger balance for automated bi-weekly payout.                                                                                                           | Protects guardians from tutor no-shows; protects tutors from payment evasion.                                                |
| 6   | **Commission & Accounting Treatment**         | Platform charges an 8% commission on lesson bookings. All money movements are recorded in an immutable, double-entry financial ledger (`ledger_accounts`, `ledger_journals`, `ledger_entries`) storing minor units (poisha = BDT * 100).                                                                                                               | Full financial auditability, zero rounding error, zero reliance on floating-point arithmetic.                                |
| 7   | **Cancellation, No-Show & Refund Matrix**     | **Guardian Cancellation:** >24h before session = 100% refund; 4h–24h = 80% refund (20% compensation to tutor); <4h = 0% refund (100% charged). **Tutor Cancellation:** 100% refund to guardian + penalty flag on tutor reliability score. **No-Show:** Tutor no-show = 100% refund + replacement priority; Student no-show = 100% charged to guardian. | Fair, deterministic compensation rules preventing late cancellations.                                                        |
| 8   | **Tutor Replacement Guarantee**               | If a tutor cancels repeatedly (>2 sessions in a month) or if the guardian requests a replacement within 48 hours of the first trial session, the platform matching team assigns an equivalent verified tutor at no extra charge or issues a 100% refund credit.                                                                                        | Core value proposition over informal coaching.                                                                               |
| 9   | **Recurring Schedules & Timezones**           | All database timestamps stored in UTC (`timestamptz`). User interfaces display and accept inputs in `Asia/Dhaka` (UTC+6). Weekly recurring slots calculate against specific Gregorian calendar dates with Bangladesh national holiday awareness.                                                                                                       | Eliminates daylight saving / timezone drift bugs.                                                                            |
| 10  | **Session Overlap & Concurrency Control**     | PostgreSQL GiST exclusion constraint (`ex_tutor_session_overlap`) on `tstzrange(starts_at, ends_at, '[)')` for active bookings. Simultaneous booking requests for the same tutor time slot are serialized with database-level row locks (`SELECT FOR UPDATE`).                                                                                         | Eliminates double-booking bugs at the database engine level.                                                                 |
| 11  | **Data Retention & Soft Deletion**            | Soft deletes via `deleted_at timestamptz` on users, file assets, and sessions. Financial records, audit logs, and attendance records are immutable and never deleted. Anonymized data retention policy compliant with Bangladesh digital records standards.                                                                                            | Meets legal auditing standards and prevents accidental data loss.                                                            |
| 12  | **File Storage & Security**                   | Private S3-compatible object storage (e.g. MinIO in local dev, AWS S3 / Cloudflare R2 in production). File uploads use short-lived presigned PUT URLs (15 min validity) and presigned GET URLs (60 min validity). ClamAV malware scanning status tracked in `file_assets.malware_scan_status`.                                                         | Keeps backend free from heavy file proxying while preventing direct unauthorized file access.                                |
| 13  | **Direct Messaging & Minor Safety**           | Direct messaging is permitted only between active booking participants (Tutor, Guardian, and/or Student). In-app messages are monitored by automated keyword filters (phone number / external link detection to prevent disintermediation and harassment). Guardians have full visibility into messages involving their linked children.               | Safeguards student safety and platform commercial integrity.                                                                 |
| 14  | **Hosting, High Availability & SLOs**         | Modular monolith deployed via Docker containers behind Nginx reverse proxy. Target SLO: 99.9% uptime. RPO: <15 minutes (continuous WAL archiving). RTO: <2 hours. Initial pilot volume: 500 active concurrent users.                                                                                                                                   | Clean operational simplicity without the distributed complexity of microservices.                                            |
| 15  | **Admin Impersonation**                       | Omitted from initial release for security and minor safety. Read-only diagnostic views provided for customer support agents with full audit logging.                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Eliminates catastrophic privilege escalation vectors.                                                                        |
| 16  | **Analytics & Privacy Boundaries**            | Product analytics track aggregated user actions (e.g. search filters used, booking conversions) without logging raw PII (names, phone numbers, student grades, or chat contents). All analytics cookies require consent.                                                                                                                               | Privacy-by-design compliance.                                                                                                |
| 17  | **Version Verification & Upgrade Policy**     | Pin exact versions in lockfile (`pnpm-lock.yaml`). Upgrade cadence: quarterly security reviews, semver patch updates automated via CI, major framework upgrades planned in dedicated sprints.                                                                                                                                                          | Reproducible builds across development, testing, and production environments.                                                |

## 2. Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) Established

The following ADRs have been generated and documented in `docs/adr/`:

- **ADR-001**: Modular Monolith Backend Architecture with NestJS
- **ADR-002**: PostgreSQL as Canonical System Source of Truth
- **ADR-003**: Transactional Outbox Pattern for Asynchronous Events
- **ADR-004**: Double-Entry Financial Ledger for Escrow, Commissions, and Payouts
- **ADR-005**: Centralized Real-Time Notification Architecture with Socket.IO
- **ADR-006**: Turborepo Monorepo with Strict pnpm Workspaces
- **ADR-007**: Next.js App Router with Bilingual Localization (Bengali & English)
